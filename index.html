How I work

From human need to tested solution.

I use Design Thinking to understand the people affected, define the right problem, explore options, build a prototype, test it, and improve the result.

1. EmpathizeUnderstand who needs help and what they need.
2. DefineTurn what you learned into a specific problem.
3. IdeateConsider several possible ways to solve it.
4. PrototypeBuild a small version that people can react to.
5. TestCheck whether it works and what needs to change.
6. Deliver and learnPut the solution into use, track progress, and improve it.
